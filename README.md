A one-line roast for the [Midnight Fridge Roast](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-midnight-fridge-roast-fc5d44a7) bounty on make.fun: a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight — and quotes its own petty policy while doing it.

## The line

> My fridge quoted policy at me at 12:01: 'Snacks after midnight need manager approval. I am the manager. Denied.'

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
